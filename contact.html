What We Can't Help With

We're not lawyers or accountants. We can't provide legal or tax advice specific to your situation. If you need personalized guidance, we recommend consulting with:

  • A business attorney licensed in your state
  • A CPA who specializes in small business taxes
  • Your state's Secretary of State office for filing questions

Our guides are designed to give you the information you need to make your own decisions — but for complex situations, professional advice is always worth the investment.
